隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日 · 适用于 TingXu / 听序 iOS 1.0

一句话总结：听序只读取你授权的 Apple 健康耳机音频暴露数据，在本机分析；不建立账户，不上传健康数据，不出售数据，不进行广告追踪。

1. 我们读取哪些数据

在获得你的系统授权后，听序只读取 Apple 健康中的两类数据：耳机音频暴露（headphoneAudioExposure）和耳机音频暴露事件（headphoneAudioExposureEvent）。

听序不请求读取位置、心率、睡眠、病历、运动记录或其他无关健康类型，也不向 Apple 健康写入任何数据。

2. 为什么读取

这些数据仅用于在本机生成聆听时长、估算会话、能量加权平均音量、声音暴露参考、健康提示、历史统计、月度报告和年度年鉴。设备收藏、场景标签和设置用于个性化展示与个人回顾。

听序不会根据音频数据推断你在做什么，也不会生成医学诊断、人格或心理结论。

3. 数据处理与存储

健康数据在本机读取和计算，不上传至任何服务器。听序不包含账户系统、云数据库、广告 SDK、第三方行为分析 SDK 或自建购买验证服务器。

为提高读取效率，听序可能在 App 沙盒的 Application Support 中保存受完整文件保护的本地健康缓存，并将该文件排除在备份之外。该缓存不会进入 iCloud、App Group 或第三方服务。设备收藏、昵称、标签和设置使用本地数据库保存，不启用 iCloud 同步。桌面小组件只读取本地汇总快照，该快照位于受保护的 App Group 中，并排除备份。

4. 我们是否共享数据

不会。听序不会出售、出租或向第三方提供你的健康数据，不进行广告画像，也不将健康数据用于追踪。

只有当你主动选择分享图片时，分享卡才会由你通过系统分享面板发送给你选择的接收方。分享行为由你控制。听序不会自动上传或自动发布健康数据。

5. 健康参考的边界

Apple 健康提供的耳机音频暴露记录不保证完整、连续或实时。听序生成的聆听时长、估算会话、平均音量、系统高音量事件和 WHO 周度参考暴露比例均为本机分析结果，可能存在缺失与误差。

这些结果仅供个人健康参考，不构成医学诊断、治疗建议或听力安全保证。若你担心听力健康，请咨询合格的医疗专业人员。

6. 管理与删除数据

你可以在“设置 > Apple 健康”或系统健康 App 的“数据访问与设备”中随时撤销听序的读取权限。

你可以在听序的“设置 > 本地数据管理”中清除统计缓存、删除全部标签、删除全部设备收藏、重置设置或清除所有本地用户数据。删除 App 内数据不会删除 Apple 健康中的原始记录。卸载 App 会删除听序的本地沙盒数据，但不会删除 Apple 健康数据。

7. 通知与照片

若你主动启用本地提醒，听序会请求系统通知权限，并仅在设备上安排每日/每周提醒。通知内容不包含具体健康数值。当你主动保存分享卡时，听序会请求照片图库“添加”权限。听序不会读取你的照片图库。

8. 儿童

听序不是专门面向儿童设计的产品，也不会主动收集儿童个人信息。HealthKit 的可用性和授权由系统与用户决定。

9. 第三方与 Apple

听序使用 Apple 提供的 HealthKit、WidgetKit、UserNotifications、Photos 和系统分享能力。Apple 对这些系统服务的数据处理受 Apple 自身隐私政策约束。

听序不是 Apple 产品，与 Apple Inc. 无隶属关系。AirPods、Beats、iPhone、iPad、Mac、Apple Watch 等名称与商标归 Apple Inc. 或相关权利人所有，仅用于说明兼容设备。

10. 政策更新与联系

如果数据实践发生实质变化，我们会在 App 内更新本政策并修改生效日期。重大变化会在更新后再次提示。

如有隐私相关问题，请通过 App Store 产品页公布的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

ENGLISH

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026 · TingXu iOS 1.0

In short: TingXu reads only the Apple Health headphone audio exposure data you authorize, analyses it on your device, and does not create an account, upload, sell or use your health data for advertising or tracking.

1. Data we read

With your permission, TingXu reads only two Apple Health data types: headphone audio exposure (headphoneAudioExposure) and headphone audio exposure events (headphoneAudioExposureEvent). We do not request location, heart rate, sleep, medical records, workouts or unrelated health data, and we never write to Apple Health.

2. Why we read it

The data is used on-device to generate listening duration, estimated sessions, energy-averaged volume, exposure references, health notes, historical statistics, monthly recaps and a yearly almanac. Device favourites, scene tags and settings personalise the experience. TingXu does not infer what you were doing from audio data, and does not produce medical, personality or psychological conclusions.

3. Processing and storage

Health data is processed locally and is not uploaded to any server. TingXu has no account system, cloud database, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or self-hosted purchase-verification server.

A local performance cache may be stored in the app sandbox’s Application Support directory with complete file protection, excluded from backups. It is never placed in iCloud, an App Group or a third-party service. Device favourites, nicknames, tags and settings are stored locally without iCloud sync. Widgets read only a local aggregate snapshot stored in a protected App Group and excluded from backups.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or provide your health data to third parties, use it for advertising profiles or use it for tracking. A share card is sent only when you actively choose a recipient through the system share sheet. TingXu never automatically uploads or publishes health data.

5. Limits of health references

Apple Health exposure records may be incomplete, discontinuous or delayed. Listening duration, estimated sessions, average volume, system exposure events and WHO weekly reference exposure are estimates computed on-device and may contain gaps or errors. They are for personal context only, not medical diagnosis, treatment advice or a guarantee of hearing safety. Consult a qualified professional if you have concerns.

6. Access and deletion

You can revoke TingXu’s read access in iOS Settings > Health or in the Health app under Data Access & Devices. In TingXu Settings > Local Data you can clear the statistics cache, delete all tags, delete all device favourites, reset settings or erase all local user data. Deleting in-app data does not delete the original Apple Health records. Uninstalling TingXu deletes its local sandbox data but does not delete Apple Health data.

7. Notifications and photos

If you enable local reminders, TingXu requests notification permission and schedules daily/weekly notifications on-device; notification content does not include specific health values. Saving a share card requests add-only Photos permission. TingXu does not read your photo library.

8. Children

TingXu is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. HealthKit availability and authorization are controlled by the system and the user.

9. Third parties and Apple

TingXu uses Apple-provided HealthKit, WidgetKit, UserNotifications, Photos and system sharing. Apple’s handling of those services is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy. TingXu is an independent product, not an Apple product and not affiliated with Apple Inc. AirPods, Beats, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch names and trademarks belong to Apple Inc. or their respective owners and are used only to identify compatible devices.

10. Updates and contact

If our data practices materially change, we will update this policy and its effective date in the app and notify you again for material changes. For privacy questions, use the developer contact published on the TingXu App Store product page.
