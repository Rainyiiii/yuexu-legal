月序隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 13 日 · 适用版本：月序 1.0 及以上

月序把“本地优先”当作产品前提：你的经期、身体感受和用药记录默认只保存在这台设备上。我们不做账号体系，不把健康数据上传到我们的服务器，也不出售你的数据。

1. 我们是谁

月序（YueXu）是一款由个人开发者提供的经期与用药记录 App。隐私相关问题可发送邮件至 ushio0314@163.com。

2. 我们处理哪些数据

你主动记录的内容

  • 经期开始/结束日期、经血量、症状、心情、备注；
  • 药物名称、剂量、用药计划、服药记录与库存；
  • 提醒设置、外观设置、应用锁与隐私模式开关。

在你授权后才访问的数据

  • Apple 健康：经期、经期症状与用药记录。只有你点击“连接 Apple 健康”并授权后，月序才会读取或写入相应类型；你可以随时在系统“健康”App 中撤销。
  • 通知：用于本地经期与用药提醒。提醒由 iOS 在本机发送，不经过服务器。
  • Face ID / 触控 ID：仅用于应用锁。生物识别由系统处理，月序不接触也不存储生物特征数据。
  • 日历（可选）：只有你主动开启“显示节假日”并授权后，月序才会读取你选择的 Apple 日历事件标题与日期，用于在周期日历中显示节假日。月序不会创建、修改或删除日历事件，不会把经期日期写入日历，也不会上传日历内容。你可以随时关闭该功能或在系统设置中撤销日历权限。

购买信息

Pro 订阅与永久会员由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。月序只读取 Apple 验证后的交易状态（是否有效、到期时间、是否为永久会员），不接触你的银行卡或支付账号。

3. 数据存放在哪里

  • 记录保存在设备的 App 沙盒内（SwiftData 本地数据库），不设云端同步。
  • 桌面/锁屏小组件通过 App Group 读取最少的展示字段（周期天数、预计下次经期、待服药次数）；开启隐私模式后不显示用药信息。
  • 你可以主动导出加密备份（PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM）。备份文件由你保存，密码不会上传或另行保存。
  • 写入 Apple 健康的数据由系统“健康”App 管理，适用 Apple 的健康数据政策。

4. 我们不做什么

  • 不出售、出租或共享你的健康记录；
  • 不投放广告，不集成第三方广告或分析 SDK；
  • 不要求注册账号，不使用健康数据做广告画像；
  • 不把健康数据上传到我们的服务器（我们也没有这样的服务器）。

5. 保留与删除

记录会一直保存在设备上，直到你在 App 内删除、清除数据或卸载 App。卸载 App 会删除本地数据库；已导出的备份文件需要你自行删除。Apple 健康中的记录请在系统“健康”App 中管理。

6. 儿童

月序不是面向 13 岁以下儿童的产品，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了信息，请联系我们删除。

7. 你的权利

你可以在 App 内查看、修改、删除自己的记录，导出加密备份，或卸载 App 删除全部本地数据。你也可以通过邮件联系我们，询问本政策的执行情况。

8. 安全

我们使用系统的数据保护、应用锁与加密备份来降低风险。但任何设备都存在丢失或被访问的可能，请为设备设置锁屏密码，并妥善保管备份密码。

9. 政策变更

如果数据处理方式发生实质变化，我们会更新本页并修改生效日期。继续使用 App 前，请查看最新版本。

10. 联系我们

邮箱：ushio0314@163.com

YueXu Privacy Policy

Effective: November 13, 2026 · Applies to YueXu 1.0 and later

YueXu is built local-first: your period, body and medication records stay on your device by default. There is no account system, we do not upload health data to our servers, and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

YueXu is a menstrual cycle and medication tracking app provided by an individual developer. For privacy questions, email ushio0314@163.com.

2. What data we process

Information you record

  • Period start/end dates, flow, symptoms, mood and notes;
  • Medication names, doses, schedules, dose logs and inventory;
  • Reminder settings, appearance settings, app lock and privacy mode.

Data accessed only with your permission

  • Apple Health: period, cycle symptoms and medication records. YueXu reads or writes the relevant types only after you tap “Connect Apple Health” and grant access. You can revoke access at any time in the system Health app.
  • Notifications: used for local period and medication reminders. They are sent by iOS on your device and do not pass through our servers.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: used only for app lock. Biometric authentication is handled by the system; YueXu cannot access or store your biometric data.
  • Calendar (optional): only after you enable “Show holidays” and grant full calendar access does YueXu read the titles and dates of the Apple calendars you select, so it can show holidays in the cycle calendar. YueXu never creates, edits or deletes calendar events, never writes period dates to your calendar, and never uploads calendar contents. You can turn this off or revoke access at any time.

Purchase information

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple’s App Store. YueXu only reads Apple-verified entitlement status (active, expiration date, lifetime access). We never receive your payment card or account credentials.

3. Where data is stored

  • Records are stored in the app sandbox on your device (a local SwiftData database). There is no cloud sync.
  • Home screen and lock screen widgets read a minimal set of display fields through an App Group (cycle day, expected next period, pending dose count). When privacy mode is on, medication details are hidden.
  • You may export an encrypted backup (PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM). You choose where to save it; the password is never uploaded or stored separately.
  • Data written to Apple Health is managed by the system Health app and subject to Apple’s health data policies.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your health records;
  • We do not show ads or integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs;
  • We do not require an account or use health data for advertising profiles;
  • We do not upload health data to our servers (we do not operate such servers).

5. Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear your data or uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes the local database; exported backup files must be deleted by you. Records in Apple Health are managed in the system Health app.

6. Children

YueXu is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete your records in the app, export an encrypted backup, or uninstall the app to delete all local data. You may also email us with questions about how this policy is applied.

8. Security

We use system data protection, app lock and encrypted backups to reduce risk. Any device can be lost or accessed by someone else; please set a device passcode and keep your backup password safe.

9. Changes to this policy

If our data practices change materially, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Please review the latest version before continuing to use the app.

10. Contact us

Email: ushio0314@163.com
